Changed your mind (7-day cooling-off)

Because you buy online, the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act lets you cancel within 7 days of receiving your goods, without giving a reason. Return the item unused and in its original packaging; you pay only the cost of returning it.

Faulty or not as described

Under the Consumer Protection Act, if an item is defective or not what was described, you may return it within 6 months of delivery for a repair, replacement or refund, at no cost to you.

How to return something

  1. Email {{ seller.email }} with your order number and the reason.
  2. We'll confirm the return address with the vendor.
  3. Refunds go back to your original payment method within 10 business days of the item arriving back.

Cancelling before delivery

Unpaid orders cancel automatically. To cancel a paid order before it ships, contact us as soon as possible and we'll refund you in full.
