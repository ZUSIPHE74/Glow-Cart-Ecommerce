Who you are buying from

Products on GlowCart are sold by the vendor named on each product page. {{ seller.name }} runs the marketplace and collects payment on the vendors' behalf. Contact details are on our contact page.

Prices and payment

  • Prices are set and charged in South African rand (ZAR), including VAT where the vendor is VAT-registered.
  • Prices shown in other currencies are estimates using daily exchange rates; your bank converts the rand amount.
  • Payments are processed by PayFast. We never see or store your card details.
  • An order is confirmed once PayFast confirms payment. Unpaid orders are cancelled after {{ unpaid_timeout }} minutes and the stock is released.

Delivery

Vendors ship paid orders to the address you give at checkout and update the order status as it progresses. You can follow every order under My orders.

Cancellations, returns and refunds

See our returns and refunds policy, which sets out your rights under the Consumer Protection Act and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act.
